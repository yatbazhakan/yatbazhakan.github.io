02 — Funding & eligibility

DfE / EPSRC studentships & Home fee eligibility

Queen's University Belfast is a Russell Group university with competitive annual doctoral studentship competitions.

UK and Republic of Ireland nationals qualify for Home studentship status at Queen's University Belfast.

Key Eligibility Advantage · CTA

UK & Republic of Ireland Home Status

Under Common Travel Area and DfE/EPSRC residency rules, both UK nationals and Republic of Ireland nationals (as well as candidates holding UK settled or pre-settled status) qualify as Home students at Queen's University Belfast and do not require a student visa.

DfE & EPSRC Awards

Competitive Home Studentships

Competitive Department for the Economy (DfE) and EPSRC Postgraduate Studentships cover 100% of Home tuition fees plus an annual tax-free maintenance stipend of approx. £21,805 per year (3–3.5 years), offering strong purchasing power in Belfast.

Early preparation · BSc / MEng / MSc

Final-Year Students Encouraged

You do not need to wait until your degree is completed to apply. Final-year undergraduate (BSc/BEng/MEng) and Master's students expecting a First or Upper Second-Class (2:1) degree are warmly encouraged to get in touch ahead of the 5 February 2027 deadline.

International rules · Max 2 projects

International & External Scholarships

A small, highly competitive pool of International scholarships is allocated across EEECS (strict School cap: maximum 2 project applications across EEECS, with complete BSc/MSc transcripts uploaded at submission). External sponsorships (e.g., government awards, MSCA, TÜBİTAK) are also supported.

04 — Mentorship

Supervision approach & candidate profile

What you can expect

  • Close collaborative supervision: Weekly technical meetings, shared codebases, and hands-on mentorship alongside faculty co-supervisors and postdoctoral research fellows.
  • Compute, simulation & multi-sensor setups: Access to HPC GPU clusters, CARLA digital-twin simulation pipelines, and multi-sensor robotics and perception setups (LiDAR, camera, 4D imaging radar, GNSS-RTK/INS, ROS 2).
  • European & UK consortia links: Active research links across Horizon Europe and UKRI programmes (CONCENTRA, Pre-LSDemo, HIVEMIND, Hi-Drive, and the UK QEPNT Hub).
  • Publication mentorship: Hands-on support targeting leading peer-reviewed venues (IEEE T-ITS, IEEE T-IV, ACM TIST, CVPR, ECCV, ITSC).

What we look for on day one

  • Academic background: A First Class or Upper Second-Class (2:1) Honours degree (or 2:2 plus a strong Master's) in Computer Science, EEE, Robotics, Artificial Intelligence, Applied Mathematics, or Physics.
  • Programming proficiency: Solid programming skills in Python (e.g., PyTorch, NumPy) and/or C++.
  • No prior AV/ROS 2 gatekeeping: Prior experience with autonomous vehicles, ROS 2, or LiDAR hardware is not required beforehand — you will build these domain skills during the PhD.
  • Curiosity and rigour: Genuine motivation to question assumptions, design reproducible experiments, and work on safety-critical AI for real-world systems.

05 — Application process

How to enquire & apply

Deadline for October 2027 entry: Friday, 5 February 2027.

  1. 01

    Informal Email Enquiry (Recommended)

    Before submitting (especially for international candidates), email {{ a.email }} with the subject line [PhD 2027 - EEECS27/HY1] or [PhD 2027 - EEECS27/YH2], attaching:

    • Your CV (plus Google Scholar or GitHub link if available)
    • Academic transcripts and degree grades (undergraduate and postgraduate)
    • Confirmation of your fee status (Home UK/Irish/Settled vs. International)
    • A short paragraph on why the project interests you and any coding or research projects you have built
  2. 02

    Fit Discussion & Scope Alignment

    Where there is strong alignment, we arrange a brief online chat to discuss how your interests fit the project scope and confirm eligibility rules ahead of submission.

  3. 03

    Formal Application via QUB Portal (by 5 February 2027)

    Submit your formal application via the Queen's University Belfast Postgraduate Applications Portal, quoting the exact project Reference Number (EEECS27/HY1 or EEECS27/YH2) and uploading complete academic transcripts. Direct portal applications submitted before 5 February 2027 are also welcome and considered for interview.
