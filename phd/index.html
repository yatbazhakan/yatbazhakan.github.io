01 — Funding & eligibility

Fully funded PhD studentships (DfE & CTA)

Queen's University Belfast is a Russell Group research institution with competitive annual doctoral funding routes

Republic of Ireland & UK citizens qualify for domestic Home fee rates under the Common Travel Area.

Key Eligibility Advantage Common Travel Area (CTA)

Republic of Ireland & Common Travel Area

Under the UK–Ireland Common Travel Area agreement, Republic of Ireland (ROI) citizens pay domestic UK Home tuition fees at Queen's University Belfast and do not require a student visa. This provides a direct, cost-effective route for Irish and eligible EU residents.

Annual competition Department for the Economy (NI)

DfE Northern Ireland Postgraduate Studentships

The Department for the Economy (DfE) funds competitive annual PhD studentships covering 100% of approved tuition fees plus an annual tax-free living stipend (currently over £19,000/year). Open to eligible UK, ROI, and EU candidates meeting residency criteria.

Early preparation BSc / MEng / MSc

Final-Year Students & Early Inquiries

You do not need to wait until your degree is finished to get in touch. Final-year undergraduate and Master's students expecting a First or 2:1 are encouraged to contact me during autumn/winter so we can prepare a strong proposal ahead of competition deadlines.

Global applicants International & External

International & External Scholarships

International candidates can apply for university-level tuition fee awards. I also actively support strong candidates applying with external scholarships (e.g., home government sponsorships, national research councils, Marie Skłodowska-Curie networks, TÜBİTAK).

03 — Mentorship

Supervision approach & prerequisites

Hands-on mentorship with dedicated compute and top-tier venue targets.

What you can expect from me

  • Regular one-to-one supervision: Weekly or fortnightly meetings with dedicated focus on your research progress and career goals.
  • Technical mentorship: Hands-on guidance in DNN introspection, neural activations, LiDAR 3D object detection, and AI safety.
  • Compute and benchmark access: Access to high-performance GPU compute clusters and key benchmark datasets (nuScenes, KITTI, Waymo).
  • Publication strategy: Active support in submitting to leading peer-reviewed venues (IEEE T-ITS, IEEE T-IV, CVPR, ICCV, ITSC).
  • Timely, constructive feedback: Thorough reviews of paper drafts, dissertation chapters, and presentation slides.

What I look for in candidates

  • Strong academic background: A First Class or Upper Second Class (2:1) Honours degree (or equivalent Master's) in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or Electrical & Electronic Engineering.
  • Programming proficiency: Strong Python skills and hands-on familiarity with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch strongly preferred).
  • Curiosity and initiative: Willingness to read papers critically, question assumptions, and drive research questions forward.
  • Communication skills: Clear written and spoken English, essential for research manuscripts and conference presentations.
  • Safety-critical focus: Genuine motivation to work on trustworthy and robust AI for real-world automated systems.
04 — Application process

How to inquire & apply

A transparent three-step route from initial email to formal QUB Direct submission

Use the subject prefix so your inquiry is routed and reviewed promptly.

1

Informal Email Inquiry

Send an email to {{ a.email }} with the subject line [PhD Inquiry] Your Name — Proposed Strand. Please attach:

  • Your current CV (including degree grades, GPA, and graduation date)
  • Academic transcripts (undergraduate and postgraduate)
  • A brief summary (1–2 paragraphs or 1 page) of your research interests and why you want to work on this topic
2

Discussion & Proposal Alignment

If there is strong research alignment, we will arrange an online video call to discuss your interests, refine a project outline, and determine the most suitable funding competition (DfE, university scholarships, or external awards).

3

Formal Application via QUB Direct

Submit your formal application through the Queen's University Belfast Direct Admissions Portal, naming Dr. Hakan Yekta Yatbaz as your proposed primary supervisor. The formal application requires reference letters, verified degree certificates, and the agreed research proposal.
