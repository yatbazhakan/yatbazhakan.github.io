Doctoral Supervision · Queen's University Belfast (EEECS)

PhD in Autonomous Systems & AI Safety

A ring-fenced, fully funded DfE PhD studentship is available in my group at Queen's University Belfast (School of EEECS), working on 3D Perception, Self-Correcting Autonomous Driving, Vision-Language Models, and Robotics. Directly open to UK/NI Nationals, Republic of Ireland (Common Travel Area) citizens, and candidates with UK Settled Status / Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR), alongside competitive international nominations.

01 — Funding & eligibility

Fully funded PhD studentships (DfE, CTA & UK Settled Status)

Clear, transparent funding routes depending on your fee status — including a dedicated Home-fee DfE studentship ready to be awarded without a school-wide quota lottery.

UK/NI nationals, Irish (CTA) citizens, and holders of UK Settled Status or ILR qualify directly for the ring-fenced Home studentship.

Ring-Fenced Slot · Home / Settled Status / ILR

Dedicated DfE Northern Ireland Studentship

I hold a directly allocated 3-year DfE studentship covering 100% of Home tuition fees plus a tax-free, NI-free stipend (∼£19,237–£20,780/year, equivalent to a ∼£25k–£27k taxed salary, with zero student loan repayments plus paid lab demonstrating top-ups). Directly open to UK/NI nationals and candidates holding EU Settled Status, Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR), or eligible 3-year UK residency.

Key Eligibility Advantage · CTA

Republic of Ireland & Common Travel Area

Under the UK–Ireland Common Travel Area agreement, Republic of Ireland (ROI) citizens qualify for domestic UK Home fee status at Queen's University Belfast without a visa. For graduates from Dublin, Galway, Cork, Limerick, or Maynooth, a tax-free UK stipend in Belfast offers significantly stronger purchasing power than Dublin living costs, just two hours away by train.

Industry Pivot & Cross-STEM · BSc / MEng / MSc

Software Engineers & Final-Year STEM Students

Graduate software/data engineers in Belfast, Great Britain, or Ireland looking to trade routine enterprise software for hands-on frontier AI R&D are strongly encouraged to reach out. Alongside CS and Electrical/Electronic Engineering, I actively welcome Physics, Applied Mathematics, and Mechanical/Aerospace Engineering graduates with strong Python and mathematical foundations.

International Quota & External Awards

International Fee-Waiver Nominations (High Bar)

International candidates can be nominated for the School's international scholarship competition. Please note: because international fee-waiver awards are strictly capped across the School (≤5 places across 30+ academics), nominated international candidates must hold an exceptional Master's track record with multiple published/accepted peer-reviewed papers (e.g., IEEE/ACM transactions or top vision/robotics venues) to be competitive, or apply with external funding (CSC, TÜBİTAK, MSCA, government sponsorships).

03 — Mentorship

Why join my group & what I look for

What you can expect from me

  • Direct 1-on-1 PI mentorship: You will not be lost in a 25-person lab or handed off to a busy postdoc. We sit down weekly for hands-on problem solving, experiment design, and paper writing.
  • Day-1 working codebase & hardware: Step straight into a production-ready PyTorch stack with pre-extracted large-scale benchmarks (nuScenes, Argoverse 2, KITTI, Vision-Language Models), GPU compute, and QUB's instrumented autonomous research vehicle — no spending 18 months fighting broken code alone.
  • Top-tier publication & travel support: Active co-authorship targeting premier venues (CVPR, ICRA, CoRL, IEEE T-ITS, IEEE T-IV, ITSC) with travel funding to present your papers internationally.
  • Summer industry research internships: Full encouragement and flexibility to undertake paid 3-month summer research internships at leading autonomous driving, robotics, and AI labs.
  • Fast, constructive feedback: Same-week turnaround on manuscript drafts, code reviews, and presentation slides.

What I look for in candidates

  • Strong quantitative degree: A First Class or strong 2:1 Honours degree (or Master's) in Computer Science, AI/ML, Robotics, Electrical & Electronic Engineering, Physics, Applied Mathematics, or Mechanical/Aerospace Engineering.
  • Coding & mathematical fluency: Solid Python skills, linear algebra/probability foundations, and comfort working with PyTorch and Linux. (Prior autonomous driving experience is not required!)
  • For Home / CTA / Settled Status / ILR applicants: Prior publications are not required — I look for curiosity, clean coding habits, and the drive to build real-world AI systems.
  • For International fee-waiver nominees: Due to the strict School-wide cap (≤5 awards across 30+ staff), nominees must already have published or accepted peer-reviewed papers alongside a top GPA to win a competitive slot.
  • Clear communication: Ability to explain technical ideas clearly and collaborate openly when debugging hard problems.
