Research Strands

Doctoral Research Strands Open for Supervision

I welcome PhD inquiries from motivated prospective students with backgrounds in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or Electrical & Electronic Engineering. Proposals can align with the following core research strands or propose related investigations:

STRAND 01

Robust Sensing for AI

Quantifying and mitigating perception degradation in adverse operating conditions (weather, sensor noise, occlusion, degradation) and domain shifts through resilient multi-modal sensor fusion and adaptive processing.

STRAND 02

Run-Time Monitoring & Introspection

Investigating neural activation dynamics, epistemic uncertainty estimation, and real-time failure detection to ensure deep learning perception operates reliably and safely under out-of-distribution conditions.

STRAND 03

Cooperative Autonomy

Developing multi-agent collaborative perception frameworks, spatial filtering, and resilient coordination mechanisms to ensure fault tolerance in Connected and Automated Vehicles (CAVs) and multi-agent systems.

STRAND 04

Explainable AI (XAI)

Designing compact, explainable neural activation encodings and interpretable latent representations for real-time introspective safety monitoring, transparency, and accountability in autonomous and intelligent systems.

Funding & Eligibility

Information for UK, EU, and International Applicants

Queen's University Belfast is a prestigious Russell Group institution offering competitive postgraduate research studentships and scholarships. Key pathways include:

Republic of Ireland & Common Travel Area (CTA)

Under the UK-Ireland Common Travel Area agreement, Republic of Ireland (ROI) citizens pay Home (UK domestic) tuition fees at Queen's University Belfast and do not require a student visa. This provides a straightforward, cost-effective route for Irish and eligible EU residents.

DfE Northern Ireland Postgraduate Studentships

The Department for the Economy (DfE) funds competitive annual PhD studentships at QUB covering 100% of approved tuition fees plus an annual tax-free living stipend (currently over £19,000/year). Open to eligible UK, ROI, and EU candidates meeting residency criteria.

QUB International Scholarships & External Grants

International and EU candidates can apply for university-level tuition fee awards. I also actively support competitive candidates applying with external scholarships (e.g., home government sponsorships, national research councils, Marie Skłodowska-Curie networks, TÜBİTAK).

Visiting Researchers & Erasmus+ Exchanges

I welcome doctoral students and Master's thesis interns from European institutions seeking 3–6 month collaborative research visits or lab placements in Belfast focused on AI safety and autonomous perception.

Supervision Details

Supervision Approach & Prerequisites

Supervision Approach

  • Regular one-to-one supervision meetings (weekly or fortnightly)
  • Technical mentorship in DNN introspection, autonomous systems, and AI safety
  • Access to high-performance GPU compute clusters and benchmark datasets (nuScenes, KITTI, Waymo)
  • Guidance on peer-reviewed publications and international conference participation (CVPR, ICCV, ITSC, IV)
  • Constructive, timely feedback on research manuscripts and dissertation writing

Prerequisites & What I Look For

  • Strong degree (First or Upper Second Class / equivalent) in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics, or related fields
  • Solid Python proficiency and familiarity with deep learning frameworks (PyTorch preferred)
  • Curiosity and willingness to engage deeply with peer-reviewed literature
  • Self-motivation and ability to manage research milestones independently
  • Strong written and spoken communication skills in English
  • Genuine passion for safety-critical AI and autonomous perception
Application Process

How to Inquire & Apply

To ensure a fast and constructive response, prospective applicants are encouraged to follow this three-step process:

1

Informal Email Inquiry

Send an introductory email to h.yatbaz@qub.ac.uk with the subject line [PhD Inquiry] Your Name - Proposed Area. Please attach your CV (with GPA/degree class), academic transcripts, and a brief (1–2 page) summary of your research interests or proposal.

2

Research Alignment & Interview

If there is strong alignment with ongoing lab research and upcoming studentship cycles, we will arrange a video call to discuss potential project scope, refine your research proposal, and identify the most suitable funding competition.

3

Formal Application via QUB Direct

Submit your formal application through the Queen's University Belfast Direct Admissions Portal, naming Dr. Hakan Yekta Yatbaz as your proposed primary supervisor. The formal application requires reference letters, verified degree certificates, and an agreed research proposal.
