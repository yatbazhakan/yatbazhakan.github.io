About

Academic Profile & Research Focus

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) in Autonomous Systems in the School of Electronics, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science (EEECS) at Queen's University Belfast. My research is about making AI in autonomous and intelligent systems safe, reliable, and trustworthy, across four strands: robust sensing for AI in autonomy, run-time monitoring and introspection, cooperative autonomy, and explainable AI.

Before joining QUB, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at WMG, University of Warwick (Feb–Sept 2025), working on the EU-funded EVENTS and Hi-Drive projects, integrating self-assessment into real-world automated driving pipelines. I completed my PhD in Engineering at Warwick (supervised by Dr. Roger Woodman and Prof. Mehrdad Dianati) and hold an MSc and BSc in Computer Engineering from Middle East Technical University (METU NCC). I serve as an Associate Editor for Connected Mobility and Automation in Frontiers in Future Transportation.
